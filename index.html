How it works

You'll see a series of scenarios starting with "What do you think about…". For each, choose one of four answers:

  • No Way! Not for me
  • I need to think Maybe, but I'm not sure
  • Maybe once I'd try it
  • Anytime! I'm into it

How your answers will be sorted

When you're both done, every scenario lands in one of three places. You'll only ever see the first two.

To-Do List

Scenarios you both said Maybe once or Anytime! to.

To Discuss

Scenarios where at least one of you said I need to think (and no one refused). Worth a conversation.

Hidden

Anything either of you said No Way! to. Never revealed to the other partner.
